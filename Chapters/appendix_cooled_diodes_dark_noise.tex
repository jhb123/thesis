\label{chapter:pd_appendix_pd_noise}
The spectra in this appendix were measured for the experiments reported on in Chapter~\ref{chapter:2um_photoiode_chapter}. The dark noise of each diode was measured as a function of reverse bias; these spectra are shown in Section~\ref{sec:pd_appendix_bias_dark_noise}. The dark noise as a function of temperature for each diode was measured at the maximum reverse bias stated in their datasheets; these spectra are shown in Figure~\ref{sec:pd_appendix_temp_dark_noise}. Section~\ref{sec:appendix_InSb} contains the measurements of the noise of the photocurrent generated by the P5968 InSb detector as a function of reverse bias.
