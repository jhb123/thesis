Thoughts on 50:50ness of beam splitter
\begin{itemize}
	\item total light the same regardless of splitting ratio
	\item contrast of MZ worse with unequal splitting ratio
	\item worse contrast = low signal (by factor of contrast)
	\item same total light, same noise as optimal splitting ratio
	\item so SNR lower
	\item Ken experiment looked at making it 50:50, need to check what measurement would be nice in this context
	\item better 50:50 subtraction, better electronic noise potential from higher TIA gain
\end{itemize}

Thoughts on CMMR. is this noise common to both photodiodes, or both paths of the MZ.
\begin{itemize}
	\item A common mode signal is a signal that is common to both photodiodes. A simple thought experiment:
	\subitem the power in both arms of the MZ increases
	\subitem the beamsplitter has equal splitting ratio (50:50)
	\subitem there is an ND filter infront of one of the photodiodes, reducing the amount of light that gets there by half
	\subitem the signal is recovered by putting x2 gain on this photodiode. 
	\subitem the CMRR is recovered
	\subitem need maths to show this
\end{itemize}
